Runbook: Database Failover Procedure
Step 1: Confirm primary node is unreachable using `pg_isready`.
Step 2: Check replica lag on the secondary node.
Step 3: Initiate failover by promoting the replica: `touch /tmp/postgresql.trigger.5432`.
Step 4: Update application connection strings (DNS or environment variables).
Step 5: Verify new primary is accepting write connections.
Escalation: Contact the DBA On-call.
