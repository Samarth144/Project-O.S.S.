Runbook: Identity Provider (IdP) Issues
Step 1: Attempt to log in via a test account to reproduce the issue.
Step 2: Inspect SSL certificate expiry date using `openssl s_client -connect idp.aegis.io:443`.
Step 3: Check Cert-Manager logs for any renewal failures.
Step 4: Manually trigger renewal if necessary.
Step 5: Clear Gateway cache to ensure new certificate is served.
Escalation: Contact Security Operations.
