Runbook: Payment Service Troubleshooting
Step 1: Verify Payment Gateway status via health check endpoint (/health/payments).
Step 2: Check connection pool metrics in Grafana (Prometheus datasource).
Step 3: If pool is exhausted, scale the Payment Service to 5 replicas.
Step 4: If timeouts persist, check if the external gateway is responding within 2s.
Step 5: Notify the #payment-alerts Slack channel.
Escalation: Contact the Fintech Platform Team on PagerDuty.
