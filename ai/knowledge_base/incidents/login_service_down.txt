Incident Title: Login Service Down (INC-103)
Root Cause: Expired SSL certificate on the identity provider (IdP) gateway.
Impact: Users were unable to log in via OIDC for 2 hours. Active sessions remained unaffected.
Resolution: SSL certificate was renewed and auto-renewal was configured via Cert-Manager.
Lessons Learned: Monitoring for certificate expiration must be integrated into the alerting system.
